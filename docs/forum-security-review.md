# Forum security and abuse review

2026-10-06. The forum was added (PLAN.md §204, §205) after `docs/security-review.md` was written, so it was not covered there. This is a read of all of it: `lib/forum/{rules,posts,http,text}.ts`, the nine routes under `app/api/forum`, `components/Discussion.tsx`, `components/StartThread.tsx`, the two forum pages, and what the rest of the site does with forum data (feeds, sitemap, search, Open Graph, JSON-LD, privacy, export, deletion). Each real finding has a fix and a test in `tests/forum-security.test.ts` that fails on the old code (verified by reverting the source: 8 of 8 fail). Things that are an owner's decision or could not be shown wrong are under "Left" and were not changed. `scripts/vendor-*.ts` were not touched.

## Found and fixed

| # | Finding | Where | Fix | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Several invisible characters survived cleaning.** Bidi isolates (U+2066 to U+2069), the Arabic letter mark, soft hyphen, combining grapheme joiner, variation selectors, tag characters, line and paragraph separators, Hangul and Braille fillers were not removed. Text could be reordered in a post, and a post or title made only of fillers (U+3164, U+2800) passed as non-empty and showed as a blank line. | `lib/forum/rules.ts:27` (`INVISIBLE`) | The set is widened; a post of only fillers is now `empty`. | "invisible tricks" |
| 2 | **No limit on combining marks.** A "Zalgo" smear of thousands of marks on one letter passed the length count (marks count as characters, but 2,000 of them are one glyph that overflows the line and covers neighbouring text). | `lib/forum/rules.ts:30` (`MARK_RUN`, `MAX_MARKS`) | Runs of more than three marks are cut to three (Arabic with shadda and a vowel is untouched). | "a smear of combining marks" |
| 3 | **The phone-number and link checks missed other scripts.** `\d` is ASCII only, so a number in Arabic-Indic digits (the natural way to write one on an Arabic site) or full-width digits passed; a link written full-width (`ｗｗｗ．ｓｐａｍ．ｃｏｍ`) or with an ideographic full stop passed the "no links" rule. | `lib/forum/rules.ts:33,43,51` | `\p{Nd}` with the `u` flag; the link test also runs on the NFKC form with ideographic stops made dots. | "digits of any script ..." |
| 4 | **A report's note and an editor's reason were not length-limited.** `checkText(x, {max})` returns the cleaned text even when it is too long, so the note (meant to be 300) and the hide reason (200, also copied into the activity log) were stored at whatever length fitted in the 16 KB request, 20 reports a day each. | `lib/forum/posts.ts:185,205` | Cut to `NOTE_MAX` (300) and `REASON_MAX` (200) characters. | "a report's note ..." |
| 5 | **Four accounts made a minute ago could hide anyone's post.** Reporting needed no account age (writing needs five minutes), so four sign-ups (five per hour per address) silenced any post, and its author's name, until an editor looked. | `lib/forum/posts.ts:187-193`, `lib/forum/rules.ts` (`AUTO_HIDE_MIN_AGE_MS`) | Only reporters whose account is at least a day old (and not disabled) count toward the automatic hide. All reports are still stored and shown to editors. | "four brand-new accounts ..." |
| 6 | **A general thread's title and starter outlived the post they belong to.** A title is words of the first post, but it stayed on the board and in the page `<title>` after an editor or four reporters hid that post, after the author withdrew it, and after the author deleted the account (which promises the words are wiped). | `lib/forum/posts.ts:43-47` (`THREAD_SQL`), `app/[locale]/forum/page.tsx`, `app/[locale]/forum/[id]/page.tsx` | The title and starter name are returned only while the first post is visible; the pages show "[removed]" (Arabic added) instead. Restoring the post brings the title back. | "a thread's title and starter ..." |
| 7 | **Editing had no rate limit.** An author could edit a post without limit during the 15 minutes (a write each time), unlike writing. | `lib/forum/posts.ts:164`, `lib/accounts/guard.ts:47,50` (`forumEdit`) | Ten edits in ten minutes per user, spent only by an otherwise acceptable edit. | "editing is limited ..." |
| 8 | **The editors' queue listed withdrawn posts.** After the author withdrew a reported post, its open reports stayed in the queue as an empty entry nobody could act on (hide and restore refuse a withdrawn post). | `lib/forum/posts.ts:229` | The queue skips withdrawn posts. | "the editors' queue ..." |

## What was checked, and found sound

- **Stored XSS.** Post bodies, titles, usernames and report text only ever reach the page as React text (`Discussion.tsx:132`, `forum/page.tsx`, `forum/[id]/page.tsx`); the forum has no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, no markdown, no `href` built from user text (thread links use the numeric id), and `dir="auto"` only sets direction. Title in `<title>`/Open Graph is escaped by Next's metadata. No JSON-LD, RSS, ICS, search index, sitemap or share-card code references the forum (grep over `lib`, `app`: only the forum's own files, nav, smoke, privacy, export, store). Usernames are `[A-Za-z0-9_]{3,24}`, unique ignoring case, so there are no homoglyph, RTL or zero-width names.
- **Links, images, tracking pixels.** None are rendered: plain text, and any link-like text is refused on write (see fix 3). No `rel`/scheme question arises because there is nothing to link.
- **CSRF and origin.** Every mutating route (`post`, `threads` POST, `posts/[id]` PATCH and DELETE, `report`, both `moderate`) begins with `postBody` (same-origin check, 16 KB streamed body cap in bytes, JSON object only). The only GETs (`thread`, `threads`, `reports`) change nothing and answer `no-store`.
- **Authorisation.** Edit and delete require `p.user_id === user.id` (a post id belonging to someone else answers `forbidden` or `not_found`, never changes it); hide, restore, lock, queue require the editor or admin role, read from the session on the server, never from the request; there is no route that sets a role. A disabled account has no session (`userForToken` joins `disabled = 0`) and `mayPost` re-checks.
- **Limits are spent only on accepted posts**, per user and per address, for posts and threads (order: text rules, account age, duplicate, then `left()` and `take()`); a new account waits five minutes and has ten posts a day counted from the database (restart-proof, and counts withdrawn posts). Duplicate check is per user per day on a hash of normalised words.
- **SQL.** Every value is a bound parameter; the only interpolation is `moderateThread`'s column assignment, chosen from a fixed map of four literals. `NaN`, `Infinity`, huge and fractional ids bind safely and find nothing (checked with `node:sqlite`).
- **Pagination.** `page` is clamped to the real last page; `after` is a cursor with a fixed page of 30 plus one; the queue is `LIMIT 100`; thread list `LIMIT 20`. The per-request costs are one count, one indexed page, and (editors only) one indexed subquery per post.
- **Deleted and hidden content.** `listPosts` returns no body and no author for hidden or withdrawn posts, even to editors (the queue is where editors read them); hidden threads are excluded by `getThread`, `findSubjectThread`, `listThreads` and `addPost`; withdrawn posts have their body and fingerprint wiped. Hidden content is in no sitemap, feed, search, JSON-LD or image because none of those read the forum.
- **Indexing.** Every API answer carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`; both forum pages set robots noindex through `pageMetadata`; the sitemap lists no forum page; `/forum` is left out of the menu; the discussion on fighter and fight pages is loaded by the browser, so a post is never in the HTML of an indexed page (a test already holds this). The layout is `force-dynamic`, so a hidden thread is not served from a cache.
- **Moderation audit trail.** Hide and restore are logged with the editor's name and reason; thread lock, unlock, hide, show are logged; the automatic hide is logged as `forum_auto_hide` with the report count; the report rows keep who, why and the outcome (`upheld`, `dismissed`, reviewer, time). A restored post cannot be re-hidden by the same reporters (one report per person per post, whatever its status).
- **Privacy page against what is stored.** `lib/privacy.ts` declares all three forum tables and their columns (a test already fails otherwise); the export includes them; no address or user agent is stored by the forum (the limiters hold addresses in memory only).

## Left, and why (nothing here was changed)

| # | Item | Where | Why left | What would close it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Four established accounts can still hide a post** (a day old, and each costs a sign-up). An editor can restore it and the same four cannot report it again, but until then the author's words are gone from view. | `lib/forum/posts.ts:190` | Whatever the threshold, a group of that size can do it; the number is a policy choice (stated in the rules file). | Raise the threshold with traffic, require a few visible posts from a reporter, or let the author see and appeal a hidden post. |
| B | **Per-address limits trust the first `X-Forwarded-For` entry**, so a client that sets the header dodges the per-address post cap (not the per-user one). Same as item A of `docs/security-review.md`. | `lib/ai-guard.ts` | Depends on the deployment's proxy. | Take the address from a trusted proxy header. |
| C | **The three-threads-a-day limit is in memory**, so a restart or several instances reset it (accounts older than a day have no database-backed cap on threads; young accounts are held by the ten-posts-a-day database count). | `lib/accounts/guard.ts:50` | A count of threads per user in the database is easy but the restart-proof rule is a product choice. | Count `forum_threads` by `user_id` over 24 hours in `startThread`. |
| D | **The same text from different accounts is not detected** (the duplicate check is per user). A spammer with several established accounts can post one message in many threads. Links, numbers and the limits make it low-value. | `lib/forum/posts.ts:110` | A cross-account rule risks refusing real short replies ("Great fight!"). | Refuse a text longer than N characters seen from three different accounts in a day. |

**Update, later the same night:** item D is closed. A long post (30 letters or more once case, accents and punctuation are ignored) that two other people have already posted within a day is refused for the third and later (`WAVE_OTHERS`, `WAVE_MIN_CHARS` in `lib/forum/rules.ts`); short replies such as "Great fight" are exempt, which was the worry that kept it open. Simulated: fifty established accounts pasting one advert get two copies through and 48 refusals (`tests/forum.test.ts`, PLAN §212).
| E | **An author can withdraw a post after it was hidden or reported**, wiping the evidence an editor wanted, and the withdrawal is not in the activity log. | `lib/forum/posts.ts:170` | Withdrawing is the author's right and the privacy promise; the report rows and reasons remain. | Log withdrawal of a hidden post in `audit` (no words). |
| F | **An author can edit a post after it was reported** (reports stay open and count). | `lib/forum/posts.ts:158` | Honest corrections are the point of the 15-minute window. | Refuse edits once a post has an open report. |
| G | **Anonymous reads are not rate-limited** (`GET /api/forum/thread`, `threads`), each costing one or two indexed queries. | `app/api/forum/thread/route.ts` | Cost is small and bounded (30 rows). | Reuse the public API's per-address limiter. |
| H | **The privacy page says the thread title is kept**; it is now cleared with the account, but the wording (and its Arabic) was not changed. | `lib/privacy.ts:29` | Legal text is the owner's; Arabic needs a reviewer. | Add "cleared if you delete your account". |
| I | **Look-alike names** (`Ringside_Team`, `l` for `I`) are possible; only exact reserved words are refused. | `lib/accounts/users.ts:16` | Needs a policy on which names to protect. | Refuse names that contain a reserved word. |
| J | **No browser or scanner run**: this was a code read plus the test suites. | | | Run a scanner against a staging copy. |

`tests/forum-security.test.ts` (8 tests) holds the fixes; the standing suites `tests/forum.test.ts`, `tests/forum-api.test.ts`, `tests/forum-pages.test.ts` still pass.
