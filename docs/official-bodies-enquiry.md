# Sanctioning bodies enquiry (draft, not sent)

Send one to each body from your own address, with the name filled in. Contact addresses are on each site (wbcboxing.com, wbaboxing.com, ibf-usba-boxing.com, wboboxing.com); check them for the current one. Nothing from a body's pages is used beyond a plain link until a reply says yes in writing.

**To:** [the body's contact or press office]

**Subject:** May a boxing database show the [WBC / WBA / IBF / WBO]'s champions and ratings, credited and linked?

---

Hello,

I'm [YOUR NAME], [ROLE] on Ringside, a boxing database (fighter and bout records, rankings, ratings) that is [not yet public / at ADDRESS]. Your site is the authoritative place for the [body]'s champions and ratings, and I would rather show them properly than guess.

**What we would like:** on each division's page, show the [body]'s current champion and numbered ratings **as you publish them, with the date of your list, crediting the [body] by name and linking to your page for that division.** We would not alter them, and we would not show them as ours.

**What we would not do:** copy your press releases, regulations, articles or images, or show a list older than the one you have published.

1. May we show your champions and numbered ratings in that way, credited and linked? "Link only" is a perfectly good answer.
2. If yes: may we read them from your website ourselves, or would you prefer to send them (a feed, a spreadsheet, a contact)? If you prefer we read them, what is the right page and how often may we?
3. How would you like the credit worded, and is there anything you would want us never to do with them?

Thank you for the work you do.

[YOUR NAME] · Ringside · [WEBSITE OR REPO] · [EMAIL]
