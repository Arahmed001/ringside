# Transnational Boxing Rankings Board enquiry (draft, not sent)

**To:** the contact shown on https://tbrb.org/ (check the site for the current address or contact form)

**Subject:** May a boxing database credit and link the TBRB's lineal championship record?

---

Hello,

I'm [YOUR NAME], [ROLE] on Ringside, a boxing database (fighter and bout records, rankings, ratings, predictions) that is not yet public. The TBRB keeps the most careful public record of who held each lineal championship, and I would rather build on it properly than approximate it.

**What we would like:** on a fighter's page, show which division's lineal championship they held and when, **crediting the TBRB by name and linking to your page for that division**. Where our data and your succession record disagree, we would show yours as the reference and flag the difference.
**What we would not do:** copy your articles, ranking write-ups or commentary, or present your rankings as ours.

1. May we use each division's succession of champions (who, from which fight and date to which fight and date) as structured facts, credited and linked? "Link only" is a perfectly good answer.
2. Would you rather we only link to the monthly rankings and archives, use them with permission, or leave them alone?
3. If you would like to share the succession records, in what form (a page to cite, a spreadsheet, a contact)?
4. Is there anything you would want us never to do with them?

In return we can send you the list of fights in our data that decided a title, for you to check against your records.

Thank you for the work you do.

[YOUR NAME] · Ringside · [WEBSITE OR REPO] · [EMAIL]
