# Transnational Boxing Rankings Board enquiry (draft, not sent)

**To:** the contact shown on https://tbrb.org/ (check the site for the current address or contact form before sending)

**Subject:** A boxing database that would like to credit and link the TBRB: lineage and rankings history

---

Hello,

I'm [YOUR NAME], [ROLE] at [COMPANY / PROJECT]. We're building Ringside, a boxing database (fighter and bout records, division rankings, ratings, predictions). It is at prototype stage and not yet public. I'd like to ask before using anything of yours, because the TBRB's work is the most careful public record of who held the lineal championship, and I would rather build on it properly than approximate it.

**What we would like to do, and what we would not.**
- Show on a fighter's page which division's lineal championship they held and when, **crediting the TBRB by name and linking to your page for that division**.
- Where our own data and your succession record disagree, show the TBRB's as the reference and flag the difference, not overwrite it.
- We would **not** copy your articles, your ranking write-ups or your member commentary, and we would not present your rankings as ours.

**Questions.**
1. May we use the succession of champions for each division (who, from which fight and date, to which fight and date) as structured facts, with credit and a link? If you would rather we only link, that is a fine answer.
2. Are the monthly rankings and their archives something you would like us to link to only, to use with permission, or to leave alone?
3. Is there a form in which you would prefer to give us the succession records (a page we should cite, a spreadsheet, a contact who maintains them)?
4. If you spot mistakes in how we show your information, what is the best way to tell you, or for you to tell us?
5. Is there anything you would want us never to do with it?

We would be glad to share back anything useful: for example, a list of the fights in our data that decided a title, for you to check against your records.

Thank you for the work you do.

[YOUR NAME]
[COMPANY / PROJECT] · [WEBSITE OR REPO] · [EMAIL]

---
**Notes for the sender (delete before sending).**
- Nothing from TBRB is used until a reply says yes, in writing; keep the reply in the repository's `docs/` or somewhere you can find it.
- The Wikipedia champions lists (CC BY-SA) are used meanwhile, credited as "Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0)". They carry their own citations. TBRB lineage, when allowed, would be a separate source in the same table, never mixed into a Wikipedia row.
