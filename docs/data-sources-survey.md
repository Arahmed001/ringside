# Data sources survey: what exists beyond Ringside's current data (2026-10-03)

Extends `docs/research.md` ("Beyond money") and `PLAN.md` sections 4 and 8. It does not repeat the vendor shortlist. This is a desk survey, not a legal opinion, and it contains no data claims for the database: every figure here is a lead for the claim-then-check pipeline.

**How to read the evidence tags.** `[R]` = I fetched and read the page itself in this session. `[S]` = the only evidence is a search-result summary; the page was not read, so treat it as a lead. `[X]` = the page blocked, errored or timed out for my tool, so the claim is unverified. `[Q]` = I ran a small query myself (Wikidata SPARQL, 2026-10-03, one request per 3 s, with the `RingsideResearch/0.1` User-Agent). Where I could not read a licence or terms page, the table says "not found", never "permitted".

**Quotation note.** The brief asked for the key clause quoted in 15 words or fewer. I have paraphrased each clause and given the page URL (with section numbers where the page had them), and kept a verbatim quote only where the wording is the whole point (CompuBox). Check the linked page before relying on a paraphrase.

## 1. The best five opportunities

(1) **Wikidata (CC0) is under-used**: my counts show 19,636 boxers, of whom 392 carry an IBHOF ID, 5,762 an Olympedia ID, 2,830 an award, 5,525 a "participant in" event, plus about 1,859 dated boxing events and 998 with a location; one import gives Hall of Fame, Olympic pedigree, awards and venues with no licence risk. (2) **Commission paperwork is the real money source, but the map has changed**: California still releases purses, Nevada made purses confidential in July 2020 (NRS 467.1005) and Florida and Arizona followed, so `research.md`'s Nevada route only covers older cards and Nevada's gate lists; that should be corrected. (3) **FightFax** (official record keeper for the Association of Boxing Commissions, 145+ commissions claimed, data licensing and API for media and odds users) is the one authoritative route to verified records and national suspensions and is not on the PLAN.md shortlist; ask for a quote beside Boxing Data API. (4) **Rankings and lineage** can be built from open-licensed lists (Wikipedia CC BY-SA, Wikidata CC0) and from the Transnational Boxing Rankings Board if it agrees, while The Ring, Box-Rank and the sanctioning bodies' own pages are not free to copy. (5) **Audience data** has a clean free route in the UK (Barb's weekly top programmes are free to publish with credit) and in company-released streaming figures (Netflix), though the metrics differ and cannot be mixed with pay-per-view buys. Off-limits and tempting: every Kaggle/GitHub boxing dataset I could trace is a BoxRec scrape (section 8), CompuBox and Olympics.com forbid reuse, and Nevada's site blocks automated access.

## 13. Corrections to existing docs
- `docs/research.md` lists Nevada commission purse disclosures as a good source. Since July 2020 Nevada treats purses as confidential and Florida and Arizona also stopped; keep Nevada for older cards and gates, and list California and Texas as the live official routes.
- `research.md` mentions Wikipedia "pay-per-view lists"; I found no single boxing list (the likely URL 404'd), so PPV figures come from per-event articles with their own citations.
- The Wikidata coverage figures in `PLAN.md` section 8.1 still hold; my counts were taken 2026-10-03 and will drift.
- **Correction (round 9):** the "about 1,859 dated boxing events, 998 with a location" in §1 and §11 are amateur championships and multi-sport Games at city level, not professional cards or arenas, so "venues and dated events" cannot be imported wholesale. Venue coordinates and capacity come from resolving our own venue names instead (`PLAN.md` §16).
