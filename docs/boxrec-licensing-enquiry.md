Hello,

I'm [YOUR NAME], [ROLE] at [COMPANY / PROJECT]. We're building Ringside, a boxing database and analytics product for fans and analysts: fighter and bout records, division rankings, ratings, fight predictions and search. It is at prototype stage and not yet public.

I'm writing because I'd like to license data from BoxRec rather than collect it any other way. To be clear about where we stand: we have not scraped BoxRec and do not intend to. **What we're hoping to license**
1. Fighters, events and bouts: results, methods, rounds, titles, venues.
2. Corner and business relationships with dates: trainers, managers, promoters, gyms (current and historical).
3. Official and fight-night weights, including missed weights.
4. Referees, judges and scorecards.
5. Rankings and title-holder history, if available.

**How we'd use it**
- Display on our site, with whatever attribution you require.
- Our own analytics: ratings, win-probability models, trend and trainer/weight analysis built from your data.
- AI features: natural-language search and generated fighter summaries built on the data.

**Questions**
1. Do you license data to third parties, and is there a standard agreement or price structure?
2. Which of the fields above exist in a licensable form, and how far back does each go?
3. How is data delivered (API, bulk files, incremental updates), and how fresh is it?
4. May we store the data in our own database, and for how long, including after a licence ends?
5. What attribution, display and caching rules apply?
6. Are there restrictions on derived analytics, on model training, or on AI-generated text built on the data?
7. Is there a sample or evaluation licence so we can test fit before committing?
8. Are there exclusivity terms, minimum terms, or limits on how many users or requests we can serve?
