# British Boxing Board of Control enquiry (draft, not sent)

**To:** the contact on https://bbbofc.com/ (check the site for the current address; the Board's main contacts page lists them)

**Subject:** Can a boxing database show a boxer's licence status from the Board's notices, with credit?

---

Hello,

I'm [YOUR NAME], [ROLE] at [COMPANY / PROJECT]. We're building Ringside, a boxing database (fighter and bout records, rankings, ratings, predictions). It is at prototype stage and not yet public. The Board's notices are the only public record of licence conditions for British boxers, and before we use any of them I would like to ask what is acceptable, because they name individuals.

**What we would like to do, and what we would not.**
- We would like to show, on a boxer's own page, the Board's own statement of their current licence status where it affects whether they can fight (for example "suspended until [date]" or "restricted to 12-minute contests"), **with the notice linked, dated and credited to the Board**, and removed when the Board lifts it.
- We would **not** show fines, "words of guidance", calls to appear or anything about people who are not boxers, and we would not copy the minutes.
- We would not copy or scrape the Board's pages automatically without your agreement.

**Questions.**
1. May we use the status of a licensed boxer as stated in a notice in this way? If not, is there a form in which you would be content?
2. Does the Board publish, or would it provide, a structured list of current suspensions and conditions for boxers, and may it be shown publicly?
3. How should we handle medical-related conditions (such as a restriction on contest length)? We would rather not show them unless the Board is happy for us to.
4. If a boxer or the Board asks us to correct or remove a line, who should we contact, and how quickly would you expect it done?
5. Is there anything you would want us never to do with it?

Thank you for your time.

[YOUR NAME]
[COMPANY / PROJECT] · [WEBSITE OR REPO] · [EMAIL]

---
**Notes for the sender (delete before sending).** See `docs/uk-sources.md` for why the notices are not scraped. Nothing from the Board is used until a reply says yes, in writing. A boxer's licence conditions are personal data: take advice before showing any of it.
