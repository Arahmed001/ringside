# British Boxing Board of Control enquiry (draft, not sent)

**To:** the contact on https://bbbofc.com/ (the Board's contacts page lists them; check for the current address)

**Subject:** May a boxing database show a boxer's licence status from the Board's notices, with credit?

---

Hello,

I'm [YOUR NAME], [ROLE] on Ringside, a boxing database (fighter and bout records, rankings, ratings, predictions) that is not yet public. The Board's notices are the only public record of licence conditions for British boxers. Because they name individuals, I would like to ask what is acceptable before we use any of them.

**What we would like:** on a boxer's own page, show the Board's own statement of their current licence status where it affects whether they can fight (for example "suspended until [date]"), **with the notice linked, dated and credited to the Board**, and removed when the Board lifts it.
**What we would not do:** show fines, "words of guidance", calls to appear or anything about people who are not boxers; copy the minutes; or copy or scrape the Board's pages automatically without your agreement.

1. May we use a licensed boxer's status as stated in a notice in this way? If not, in what form would you be content?
2. Does the Board publish, or would it provide, a structured list of current suspensions and conditions, and may it be shown publicly?
3. How should we treat medical-related conditions (such as a restriction on contest length)? We would rather not show them unless the Board is happy for us to.
4. If a boxer or the Board asks us to correct or remove a line, whom should we contact, and how quickly would you expect it done?

Thank you for your time.

[YOUR NAME] · Ringside · [WEBSITE OR REPO] · [EMAIL]
